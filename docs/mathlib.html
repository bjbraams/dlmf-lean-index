Mathlib tagging candidates

Mathlib and TauCeti declarations that correspond to an equation of the NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions. A few already carry Mathlib's @[dlmf] attribute; the others were matched by hand in lean-DLMF, where each match is a checked dlmf_upstream pointer that fails to build if the declaration disappears. Each match records how the upstream statement relates to DLMF's: exact, stronger, special case, partial, or a different convention.

A tag is written @[dlmf 5.4.E1], optionally with a comment: @[dlmf 5.4.E1 "real argument only"]. The suggested attribute lines below put the recorded relation in that comment. They are proposals: each should be checked against the DLMF page before it goes into a pull request.

Relations are the judgement of lean-DLMF's authors and record branch, domain and normalization differences: "special case" restricts DLMF's parameters, "partial" covers one part of a multi-part equation, "different convention" differs in normalization or branch. DLMF is taken as authoritative.

Generated by scripts/build.py in dlmf-lean-index from the dump of lean-DLMF's scripts/DumpDLMF.lean. TauCeti is harvested only through the modules lean-DLMF imports.
